## Context

A drift audit on 2026-10-14 (ank 0.9.1, c859d01f) measured the tree against
the intention SPEC-1d5b44efd388 states, "the stupid coordination tool", and
found the weight elsewhere than in the loop:

- 136 256 lines of Rust in six crates; ank-core and ank-contract, which carry
  the format and the loop, are 8 991 of them, under 7 percent.
- crates/ank-tui is 28 290 lines and pulls 83 crates (`cargo tree -e normal`),
  against 38 for ank-core. The reader has had three ratified shapes and one
  proposed since 2026-08-24 (ADR-c07e2694f0e1, ADR-559eebf5c6f5,
  ADR-ac6be1ebe9aa).
- Of 80 accepted ADRs, about half decide a surface or its plumbing: the reader
  (3), the daemon (2), peers and federation (8), signers (4), distribution,
  update and release (7), the documentation pipeline (3). The other half decide
  the format, the planes, the claim, the proof and check.
- Five ADRs ratified between 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-13 had no code behind them
  and 8 of the 12 open tasks served them: hermetic verifier environments pinned
  by OCI digest, a run registry, and a verb reading signing keys from GitHub.

None of those decisions is wrong on its own. Together they describe a product,
and the specification refuses one in its first page: not a tracker, not a wiki,
not a security boundary. What was missing is the decision that says what the
surface is for, so that a new surface is measured against something.

## What differentiates ank, said once

A GitHub issue is also a task with a description and a thread. Four things are
not issues, and they are the whole of the difference:

1. **The perimeter is a path, not a label.** `ank context src/auth/` is a
   set-membership test over paths of the tree. No tracker answers "what binds
   this directory".
2. **The rule is an entity of its own.** An ADR carries a constraint, a scope
   and a ratification hash, and `context` serves it where it binds. A pinned
   issue is not that.
3. **The close is measured by the tool.** The criterion is frozen at claim and
   `done` runs the declared verifiers itself. An issue closes when somebody
   clicks.
4. **Everything is in the clone.** No API, no token, no network between an
   agent and the corpus: a fork, a worktree, a CI runner without secrets all
   hold the whole of it.

A decision that adds to the surface says which of the four it serves. One that
serves none is a product decision, and the specification already says ank is
not one.

## Decision

One executable, settled: ADR-1ea31c2f3c5a stands, and so does every decision
that put a surface inside it. What this adds is the brake. The verbs `ank help`
prints at the ratifying commit are the surface; adding a verb, a crate, a
protocol, a reader, a background process or a cross-corpus mechanism is a
supersession of this document, written against the four properties above.
Existing surfaces keep their decisions and gain nothing of that kind without
the same supersession.

## What this does not do

It removes nothing. The reader, the daemon, the protocol surface and the peers
stay exactly as ADR-8bd76e8d7c4e, ADR-24e21cb83793, ADR-fd98f4bc6dea and
ADR-a1de673043b4 state them, and a task against any of them is ordinary work.
It does not touch flags on existing verbs, which are how a verb evolves. It
does not decide ADR-ac6be1ebe9aa, which is proposed and the maintainer's to
ratify or close.

## Rejected

- **Splitting the surfaces out of the binary.** Considered on 2026-10-14 and
  refused by the maintainer: one executable is what makes a route unable to
  fail to arrive (ADR-1ea31c2f3c5a), and a second file is the distribution
  problem that ADR already paid for once.
- **A ceiling on lines or crates.** Revised at every change and silenced within
  a month, the same reasoning ADR-cc65f1388a71 gives against a process ceiling.
  Invariance of the surface is the property that holds.
- **Scoping this on `crates/**`.** It would charge every task in the workspace
  with a paragraph about surfaces (ADR-6e09f8a67540). A surface is born in the
  dispatch table and the workspace manifest, so that is where this binds.
