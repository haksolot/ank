Implements ADR-09b985a5627e, the base the rest of the plan stands on. Measured
on 2026-10-26 at e04c0dad: the claim in `web` passed with `api` on its unmerged
branch `work` and was refused with `api` on `main`; the same ADR read active or
proposed by `api`'s checkout; an uncommitted ADR draft in `api` was listed in
`web` as proposed.

The peer is opened today through `Store` over its working tree (LOG-879c2ce798a1
explains why never `Index`: `Index::open` writes index.db into the corpus it is
pointed at). Reading at a ref means reading `.ank/` out of a tree object. The
base resolution is the one `verify.rs` already uses: the local default branch,
then `refs/remotes/origin/<branch>`. Look for a reader over a tree object before
writing one; `check` replays anchors from history and may already have it.

**Count, do not time.** `git cat-file --batch` over the peer's entity files is one
process per peer; one process per file is a regression. Count the git processes
of `ank context src/` in `web` with GIT_TRACE before and after, and record both
with `ank log`. ADR-f3d1 is the rule: a verb pays for the answer it gives.

Expose the peer commit that was read from whatever opens the peer: the
execution-mode task of ADR-50952ba5e226 records it in the claim.
