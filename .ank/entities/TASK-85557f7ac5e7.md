The decision, and it is a decision rather than a fix. ADR-2f8a61c04b7d says no verb is ever added to the agent surface. This adds one, so the ADR is superseded rather than edited -- an accepted constraint is not amended in place, and the agent role cannot do it anyway.

The reason is a hole found by trying to close .ank/ to direct reads. context serves the criterion and the constraints under the budget of section 5; it never serves a task body. The bodies are where the traps live -- this one included. With cat forbidden and show human, an agent had no way left to read the reasoning it is supposed to inherit, and SKILL.md said cat is your show precisely because show was out of reach.

show is the only unbounded reader in the system, which is the argument that put it on the human side. That argument was about output size, and section 5 answers size with a budget and a truncation notice. Withholding the reasoning entirely is the worse trade.

What must not follow: eight is not a slope. The ADR replacing 2f8a has to name the surface as eight and restate that no verb is added without superseding it in turn, or the freeze becomes a formality that any task can spend.
