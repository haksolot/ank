Found while capturing the goldens for TASK-2c12b027f805, not by reading the
code: `ank init --json` prints its six human lines and no document.

`SPEC-cd0d3377b37f` says `--json` is available on every command without
exception, and calls full scriptability an invariant rather than an option.
`init` is the exception nobody noticed.

The reason it was never noticed is the interesting half, and it is what the
second clause of the criterion is for.
`no_verb_puts_anything_but_json_on_stdout_under_json` does iterate every verb
the listing offers, `init` included. But its fixture is a `Repo`, which already
carries a `.ank/`, so `init` refuses; a refusal writes to stderr and leaves
stdout empty; and `assert_json_only` returns early on the empty case. The sweep
therefore proves that `init` refuses cleanly and has never once seen it
succeed.

A sweep that only ever reaches a verb's refusal is a sweep with a hole the
shape of that verb's success.
