- Fixture of `claims_remote.rs`: `status --remote --json` 27 processes.

  two extra ones. They appear only when the read inspects the corpus again.

  `cat-file -p HEAD:.ank/archive/entities` and two `hash-object --stdin-paths`,
  beside five `cat-file --batch`.

change by the count, never by a wall.
