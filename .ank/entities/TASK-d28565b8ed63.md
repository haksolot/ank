Found by TASK-08615a199a6a and reported in its closing summary. That task made
`claim` hold on a peer edge, as ADR-c23bef1cc93e requires, but two readers of
the same fact were left behind:

- the claimable list of `ank context` still ignores peer edges, so a task
  waiting on a peer is offered as claimable and then refused by `claim`: two
  verbs disagreeing about one task;
- the "another ready task" hint printed when a claim is refused skips every
  task with a peer edge, which is safe but wrong the other way, since a task
  whose peer blocker is done is ready.

Reuse the resolution TASK-08615a199a6a wrote for `claim`, so the three answers
cannot drift apart. An unreadable peer holds, here as in `claim`.
