check_adr tests the chain without looking at the status of the ADR making the claim. A proposed ADR naming a supersedes target therefore produces a fault -- 'supersedes X, which is not marked superseded' -- from the moment the file exists.

The consequence is out of proportion to the mistake, and it is structural rather than theoretical. A fault exits 8, exit 8 fails the check-repo verifier, and check-repo is declared by nearly every task in this corpus. So proposing a succession stops ank done from working anywhere in the repository until a human runs accept -- which needs a signing key, which is TASK-313165e55d81.

An agent may write new:adr:proposed and may never accept. The role table therefore hands the agent an action whose only effect, until a human intervenes, is to block every other agent. That is the defect.

Proposed means intent. The chain is not broken until the succession is real, and it becomes real at accept.
