 'branch names nothing because nothing was ever committed' from 'default_branch is mistyped', since both are an unresolvable revision. HEAD resolving is what separates them -- no commit anywhere is the nominal state of a fresh ank init, a mistyped branch in a repository full of history is not. Without that split the drift signal would fire on every fixture in the suite that never commits.
