Measured on 2026-09-14 on this repository: `refs/ank/*` holds 664 blob
refs, 7.5 MB, and every verb moves all of it through one `cat-file --batch`
before answering. `refs/ank/proof/*` is 205 refs, 3.3 MB, 20 518
attestation entries for 205 facts -- up to 171 on one task, one per CI run,
until the attest job learned on 2026-08-30 to fetch the proofs it had
already written. The fix was in the pipeline, and a pipeline is an
integration (ADR-493471d64ba0): nothing in ank stopped the ref from taking
an identical entry forever. `refs/ank/watch/origin/*` is 148 refs, 1.4 MB,
of which the proofs are read by nobody. `refs/ank/remote-check/*` is 311
refs, 2.7 MB, in a namespace no source in this tree has ever written or
read.

## What this completes

ADR-493471d64ba0 admits a proof in a ref and says when it is pruned. It
does not say what a second attestation of the same fact does, and a record
that appends is a record that grows with the number of times a pipeline
ran. This decision closes that: one entry per fact, replaced in place, so
the size of a proof ref is the number of distinct things attested about
the task. A compaction of a ref written before this rule leaves what the
rule would have left.

ADR-24e2ecf7c0f1 has the daemon fetch `refs/ank/*` whole. The mirror
exists to answer who holds what in another clone; `status` reads its
claims and nothing reads its proofs, which arrive with the branch that
carries them. So the mirror narrows to the claims namespace. This decision
supersedes that sentence of ADR-24e2ecf7c0f1 and keeps the rest: the
daemon still answers no verb, still fetches nothing outside `refs/ank/`,
and every verb still behaves as if it were absent.

## Why a finding and not a prune

A namespace nobody serves is either a former feature or a session's
`ANK_NAMESPACE` left behind. ank does not delete what it did not write:
the reader names the refs and the command, and a human runs it, one ref by
name -- a wildcard push over `refs/ank/*` from a worktree force-reverts the
attestations a CI wrote.
