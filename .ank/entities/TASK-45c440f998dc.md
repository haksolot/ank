Follow-up to GitHub #495. Measured in a herdr pane on a corpus whose .ank/config.yml carries a duplicate 'verifiers' key: the note shows 'error[1]: .../config.yml: duplicate field verifiers' and nothing says which call was refused. Failed::Refused's Display drops 'shown' whenever stderr is non-empty (ank.rs:299), though Ank::spelling documents the spelling as 'the name in a refusal'. On #495 that spelling would have shown 'ank --repo ... find --json' and the order defect at once. Display is left alone: setting_row keeps the first line of it, and that line has to stay the CLI's error. Out of scope: the header and entity panel saying 'has not been read' / '0 in the corpus' after a refused first read (separate).
