Issue #385, side finding, reproduced on this tree at 0195eb1: sed on
done_criteria of a done task between two ank check runs produces a byte-for-byte
identical output, exit 0 both times, while the proof carries criteria:<hash>.
ADR-6b3f19e08a24 promises every freeze is "checked at the point of use"; after
done, no point of use replays it, so a hand edit is effective and invisible --
the exact fiction that ADR exists to prevent. check is the point of use for a
finished task. Severity is fault: the edit is actionable (git diff names it,
reverting clears it), unlike the dead scope ADR-3094 keeps.
