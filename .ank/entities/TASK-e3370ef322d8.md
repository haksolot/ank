`check` reports 186 entities on this corpus as written by an agent and read by
no human: 122 tasks, 33 ADRs and 31 specs. It is half of every signal the corpus
carries.

**Exactly one place in the binary writes a reading**, `promote` in `human.rs`,
reached only from `accept`. So the signal clears for an ADR or a spec at its
ratification and clears for nothing else, and a task can never carry a reading
at all: `accept` refuses a task by kind, before anything is read.

ADR-3877fef1d662 describes the field and stops there. An entity *may* carry
`verified:`, a list of readings each naming a typed actor and an instant, and
"it records that somebody read the entity and stands behind it. It is optional,
its absence is never a fault, and no verb requires it." The field is defined,
`check` derives a signal from it, and nothing on the surface writes one on
purpose. The corpus asks a question and ships no act that answers it.

**A finding nobody can clear is the shape this corpus fights everywhere else.**
`check_scope_alive` lowers the severity of a dead scope git can explain rather
than leave a reader a fault they cannot act on, and calls the alternative a
finding readers learn to skip. TASK-4c031f7b44ed exempted a closed task from the
dead-scope repair on the same reasoning, and TASK-a0ec19b32c39 did it again for
a closed chain. This is the same shape at fifty times the volume.

**A reading is not a ratification, and the verb must not borrow its ceremony.**
ADR-3877fef1d662 is explicit that the convention is a signal and not a wall: an
agent can write `human:` in front of its own name exactly as it can set
`ANK_AGENT` to anything, and what the field buys is that the ordinary case
becomes legible rather than that the dishonest one becomes impossible. So no
signature, no default branch, no refusal on who is calling. What it records is
that somebody read it, on the same terms every other typed identity here is.

**Append and never replace.** A reading is a fact about a moment, and a second
reader does not overwrite the first. The nearest shape already on this surface
is `attest`, which appends a proof and is on the surface for exactly that
reason.

The surface grows by one verb, so section 4 of the specification gains it in a
succession, and the contract version moves.
