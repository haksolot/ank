Issue #500, portability. Implements ADR-da2819aef598: **do not claim this
before it is accepted.**

A binding is resolved twice (`Peer::binds`): the reader opens the peer through
its own declaration, and the peer's `bb:**` is resolved through the peer's
declarations back to the reader. An override that fed only the first would leave
the second comparing against a path that does not exist on this machine, and the
binding would still vanish. That is why the criterion has both repositories
misplaced, and why it asserts both resolutions.

Keyed by the declaring corpus's identity (ADR-621a7fd96ce1), so the override
survives a move of the declaring checkout. Written only by `ank config --user`.
