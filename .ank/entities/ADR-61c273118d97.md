## Context

On main at daaf6c21, a healthy corpus (0 faults) printed 45 signals. About 38 of
them owe nothing, and several say so in their own message: `scope-closed` ("the
task is closed: nothing is owed"), `dead-scope` under ADR-cb68, `prose-identifiers`,
`untyped-author`, `authorless`, `discrepancy` ("the freeze is untouched"). Thirteen
lines were `burst` windows from past planning sessions. What could be acted on
(325 cold entities, five unreachable proofs, 47 signatures this machine cannot
check) was lost among them, and a reader who always sees 45 lines stops reading.

ADR-df1035934387 folds a kind that fires on many entities into one line. It compresses;
it never separates what is owed from what is merely known, which is the
question a reader of check is asking.

## Decision

Each kind declares, in one table beside the kinds, when one of its signals is
owed. The general rule: unsettled work, or a named remedy. A settled entity whose
rule names no remedy owes nothing, and a `burst` is owed only while one entity it
counts is unsettled. Nothing is hidden from a reader who asks: `--all` restores
today's output, `--kind` shows one kind whole, and `--json` keeps every finding
with its `owed` field. Folding (ADR-df1035934387) still applies within each half.
