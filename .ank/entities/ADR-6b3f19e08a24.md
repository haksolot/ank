The CLI cannot be a gatekeeper: the agent has filesystem access, and the format
is open by design. A "defended" immutability would therefore be a fiction, and
worse, a fiction people would rely on.

Concrete consequence: the freeze of `done_criteria` is anchored in the claim,
that of `constraint` and `scope` in the ratification commit, that of a verifier's
definition in the proof entry. Editing a file unblocks nothing; it only makes the
divergence visible.
