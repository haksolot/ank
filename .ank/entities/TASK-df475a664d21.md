Implements the refspec clause of ADR-b6fe84504ff2. origin's +refs/ank/*:refs/ank/* would fetch, and with fetch.prune delete, refs/ank/claims/* under the live claims of a clone that coordinates through ank. The ADR states the property; the mechanism is this task's to measure. A negative refspec (^refs/ank/claims/*, git 2.29 and later, below the 2.34 ank requires) is the candidate: measure it on all three platforms, including that prune honours it, before relying on it. Existing tests to keep green: tests/init_refspec.rs, tests/init_origin.rs.
