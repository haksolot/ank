## Context

Developers reading the README, the book and the site report that the prose
reads as generic AI output. Measured on 2026-10-26 at f997ea88, most of what
they react to is a fault a script can find, and every one of them is a
regression of a rule the corpus already holds:

- TASK-a070ea7c72f2 removed 232 em dashes on 2026-08-17 with the criterion
  "grep finds no em dash in any docs/*.md". Nothing enforces it, and 23 are
  back: format.md 13, check.md 3, quickstart.md 2, skill/plan/SKILL.md 2,
  install.md, ci.md and peers.md 1 each.
- `skill/` holds seven SKILL.md files. alternatives.md says seven at line 51
  and six at lines 75 and 79, with a token count measured over six;
  multi-agent.md:12 says five siblings; install.md shows two third-party
  captures at six and explains them with "before ank-herdr joined". The
  compare page of ank.dekrow.com says six in two sentences.
- install.md names five entity identifiers and quickstart.md seven, on the two
  pages a newcomer reads before holding a corpus. ADR-1e6bcbf62e61 keeps an identifier
  in prose a non-reference, so the corpus is indifferent; the reader is not.
- Sentences narrating the page's own history ("captured before", "used to
  live there", "used to cost it thirteen minutes") sit in install.md,
  format.md and ci-jobs.md.
- Contrastive negation, counted as `not a|never|rather than|is not|are not|
  instead of|not the` per page: format.md 80, integrating.md 48, watch.md 24,
  quickstart.md 22, mcp.md 22, install.md 20, skill/SKILL.md 19, peers.md 18.
  It is the project's voice, and in that density the first thing a reader
  names as machine-written.

The book is 43,668 words over 27 pages; doc_replay.rs already replays every
output block ank prints (ADR-2b62b9a1fe67), docs.yml already checks every
internal link and fragment, and four reference pages are generated. What is
missing is the rule for the prose around them.

## Decision

The rules are Vale rules, in a style under `.vale/`, with Vale pinned in
mise.toml and locked for the four platforms like every other tool
(ADR-9c69bd47ce23). They run from a test in `crates/ank-cli/tests/prose.rs`
through `mise x`, the route test-ank-cli already takes, which puts them in
`cargo test`, in `ank done` through the default verifiers, and in the three
required checks without a new job. docs.yml is not a required check
(ci-jobs.md) and a lint that lives only there blocks nothing.

The negation count is a budget, not a rule: the test holds a table of one
number per page, fails when a page exceeds its row, and the only way the row
moves is down, in the pull request that rewrites the page. A page nobody has
rewritten keeps its count; a page somebody rewrites cannot regress.

Not chosen: a skill under `.claude/skills/`, which CLAUDE.md's own rule
excludes (no constraint is summarised outside the corpus) and which a harness
loads probabilistically; a hook, which runs only where it was installed; and
a Rust test alone, which would re-implement what Vale does for a regex and
leave the prose rules nowhere a writer can read them as rules.

## Rules of judgement, read against a rewrite

What the lint cannot see, and what the reviewer of a rewritten page reads for:

1. A page serves one of the four sections of SUMMARY.md and one reader; a
   subject explained on two pages is the defect TASK-6113526927b2 closed.
2. A page describes the current release. History goes to an ADR or the log.
3. A command is shown after it was run, and its ank output is a replayed
   block. Output of a third-party tool is not shown: the command and one
   sentence on what it prints.
4. A "not X, Y" contrast earns its place only when X is something the reader
   believes; otherwise the sentence says what the thing does.
5. An adjective is replaced by a number or a mechanism, and a number stands
   next to the command that produced it.
6. The page ends when the content ends: no summary, no closing aphorism.
7. A fact the code does not show is not written; it is a question to the
   maintainer.

## The site

ank.dekrow.com is built from the private repository haksolot/ank-website and
carries its own copy of install, compare, multi-agent and mcp. Under
ADR-33970fcdb6e8 a copy kept outside docs/ is a satellite; the work of
replacing those four pages by the positioning page and links into the book
belongs to that repository and is planned there, not here.

## Consequences

Vale joins mise.toml and mise.lock. A contributor without mise sees the
message test-ank-cli already prints. skill/plan/SKILL.md loses two em dashes
and bumps its revision as tests/skill.rs requires. The budget table is a file
a reviewer reads in every rewrite's diff.
