Measured at the root of this repository, with exactly one proposed ADR in the
corpus:

    PROPOSED (0, non-binding)
      +1 more

The header says there are none and the next line says one was cut. Both cannot be
true, and a reader has no way to tell which is the lie.

The likely cause is that the header counts what was emitted rather than what was
found, so a section entirely eaten by the budget reports zero. Confirm that before
fixing it: if the count is correct and the truncation notice is the one that is
wrong, the fix is the other way round and the criterion still holds.

Section 5 is explicit that a truncation must say what it cut. A notice that
disagrees with its own header is worse than no notice, because it makes the reader
distrust the number they can see rather than go looking for the one they cannot.

Same family as TASK-e3d00a6e62bb, and a different code path: that one is `review`
printing no ratification queue at all, this one is `context` printing a queue that
counts itself wrong. Worth reading together and fixing separately.
