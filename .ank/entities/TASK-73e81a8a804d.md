Recorded on 2026-08-15 in LOG-c57135ae45f3 and left untasked since: `review`
and `status` filter the ratification queue on `EntityKind::Adr`, so a proposed
spec waits for a human nowhere a human looks. `accept` draws from that queue
and promotes specs, so the gap is real rather than theoretical.

It was left outside the criterion of TASK-867a9a59f265 on purpose, and the
reason given was sound: widening `review`'s **live** section is a decision of
its own, because that section counts what a constraint binds and a spec binds
nothing. That argument covers the live section and not the queue. The queue
asks which decisions are waiting for a signature, and a proposed spec is one.

Measured again on 2026-08-18, and this is what made it worth a task rather than
a second log entry. SPEC-199de7ac4730 was proposed as a supersession of
SPEC-3d12e76d9fa2. `ank find --type spec --status proposed` names it,
`check` reports it twice, and both `ank review` and `ank status` say there is
nothing proposed at all -- so the one verb whose stated job is the ratification
queue told the maintainer their queue was empty while a document sat in it.

The live section stays as it is. Nothing here asks a spec to appear among live
constraints, and the criterion says so, because the argument for keeping it out
is unchanged.
