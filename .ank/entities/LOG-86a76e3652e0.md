 most 100 characters, and otherwise holds the first segment cut at the last space at or after character 50 and at or before character 100, with the remainder going into the body verbatim between exactly one leading and one trailing newline. The message is the exact concatenation of the two, the separating space being part of the remainder, so nothing is inserted or removed on the way back and the round trip is byte for byte. Measured on this corpus: 510 entries across 177 files, mean 453 characters and longest 2105, no tab, no trailing space, every line matching the grammar. A 2105-character title would be emitted as one enormous quoted scalar and printed by every lister, which is what this avoids: the rendered line is bounded by the title and says that there is more, and the whole message reaches a caller through --json and through show. The alternative of keeping the message whole in the title and eliding at each lister was rejected: it is lossless too, but it moves the cost to five printers instead of one storage rule, and it leaves the file itself unreadable in a diff.
