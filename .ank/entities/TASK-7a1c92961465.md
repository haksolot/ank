Found while re-pointing the citations left by the two supersessions of
TASK-54764d2c16dc. `crates/ank-contract/src/verbs.rs:326` cited
`SPEC-4eff92fd80ce` as the reason for a design, hours after that document became
superseded, and nothing said so. Three holes, and each on its own is enough:

- **The walk is one crate.** `root` is `CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR` of `ank-cli` and the
  walk covers `src` and `tests` under it, so `ank-contract`, `ank-core` and
  `ank-mcp` are never read. The stale citation was in one of the three, which is
  not a coincidence: the guard has never looked there, so nothing there has ever
  been kept honest.
- **The dead list is transcribed.** Three ADR ids sit in a `const DEAD`, added by
  whoever last remembered. Superseding an entity does not add to it, so the
  guard protects exactly the three cases someone thought about and no fourth.
  A property that has to be remembered is the property this repository keeps
  replacing with one that turns red.
- **It says `adr` and means it.** Specs supersede here too, three times for the
  CLI surface alone, and the guard has no opinion about them.

The citation itself is already re-pointed; this is about the reason it survived,
which is the part that will happen again. Deriving the set from the corpus is
what makes the fix hold: the corpus knows what is superseded, and a test that
asks it needs nobody to remember.
