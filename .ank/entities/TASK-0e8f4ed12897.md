an amends written wrong can today be repaired only by hand, and `check` names
`ank show <amendment>` as the repair, which shows the problem and fixes nothing.

say it reaches an accepted spec too, because the anchor covers the body and
scope and not what the spec cites. Whether `amends` sits inside or outside the
anchor was decided by TASK-fe548f3dd587's implementation; measure it rather than
assume it, and the criterion takes either answer.
