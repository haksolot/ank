So a finding that reached for the obvious wording -- ank amend <the-expensive-ADR> --drop-scope -- would name a refusal on every line it ever printed. The finding therefore points amend at the perimeter, which is Open or InProgress by the guard above it and is amendable, and says of the heaviest constraint that it is anchored and that mechanisation is what lowers its charge.
