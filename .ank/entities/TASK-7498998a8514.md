**A tap and not homebrew-core, and the reason is measured.** Homebrew's
acceptable-formulae gate is notability — of the order of 75 stars or 30 forks,
with a track record. This repository has 1 star, 0 forks and three weeks of
history, so a core pull request would be closed with a pointer to a tap. That is
their documented door and not a judgement about the tool.

Nothing here is thrown away when the numbers change: the same formula goes to
core with its source swapped from the released archive to a build from source.

**No satellite repository.** `brew tap <user>/<name> <url>` takes an arbitrary
URL, so this repository is its own tap and ADR-e3cb36646d77 is satisfied without
being touched. The cost is that a user passes the URL once, and that is the
whole of it.

The formula must cover Intel macOS, which is why this waits on the matrix task:
a formula whose Intel branch resolves to nothing is worse than no formula, since
it fails on the user's machine rather than in CI.
