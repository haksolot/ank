Under ADR-4004eb9be5e9. The case that motivated it is PR #523, where
LOG-090ad90eb071's author was rewritten by hand on a branch and check exited 0.

The plumbing is already admitted by ADR-9307e5d214a7: merge-base for the base,
diff-tree for what the branch's commits changed, and hash-object against
cat-file --batch-check for what the working tree changed. A per-entry process
would break ADR-cc65f1388a71; the criterion counts it.
