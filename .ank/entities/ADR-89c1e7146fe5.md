Supersedes ADR-376bed7fa39a, whose text is carried forward whole; what changes is two additions to the surface, each naming the property it serves as that decision requires.

ank reject (GitHub #777): ADR-0f4248812651 decides what it does. Without it a proposal that will never be accepted has no terminal state, and a successor cannot be ratified until the rejected decision is.

ank hooks status (GitHub #790, item 3): which harnesses have ank entries installed, pointing at which binary, and which guard.* levels are in force once ANK_GUARD_* is applied. Today the only way to know is to open the harness settings file, which is the one place the hooks are meant to make unnecessary to read.

Not added: a named adapter per harness. Those are agents of ank hooks, not subcommands, and enter by amendments of ADR-43cbb20dcbf1 (ADR-b108b24202ed is the first, generic).

ADR-3b6ba766a42e holds accept until no file outside .ank/ cites ADR-376bed7fa39a; the sweep is its own task.
