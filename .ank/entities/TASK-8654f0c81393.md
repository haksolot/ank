Measured 2026-09-14, release build of 2a65d0c, this corpus: `context .`
costs 0.46-0.59 s in orientation and 0.68-0.78 s under a live claim. The
difference is `claim::bearing_on` (`claim.rs:1302`): `store.list_ids()` then
`store.load()` on every one of the 1921 entities, to keep the accepted ADRs
whose scope intersects the task. `claim::status_map` (`claim.rs:2274`) does
the same to read every task's status, and `acquire` calls it twice
(`check_blockers` at `:2298`, `other_ready_task` at `:2352`). The index rows
already carry kind, status and scope (`Index::by_status`, `by_kind`,
`read_row`).

**Callers to re-plumb with the index**: `applicable_constraints`
(`claim.rs:1256`) and `suspended_constraints` (`:1285`), reached from
`acquire` (`:2096`, an index is already open at `:2128`),
`done::warn_on_constraint_drift` (`done.rs:396`, the index opened at
`done.rs:216` is in another function) and `context::build_execution`
(`context.rs:969`, which has one). `bearing_on` filters rows with
`scopes_intersect` and loads only the survivors: an `Adr` is still needed
for its constraint and its freeze state. Fold in `show`'s second open:
`edges_of` (`human.rs:7039`) can take the index opened at `human.rs:6718`.
