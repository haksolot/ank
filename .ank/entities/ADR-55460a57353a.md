## Context

GitHub #786. Whether a finding is a fault or a signal is fixed in the binary. no-signers, self-ratified, proof-commit-unreachable and done with no test proof are the right signals for a solo corpus and are what a team under a compliance requirement needs to gate on; today the only way is a script over check --json that re-implements policy outside the corpus.

## Decision

Raise only. A repository may make itself stricter and never hide a fault, so the corpus cannot be quietly silenced through a config line, and a red check stays red. The policy is a committed file, so changing it is a diff in review, on the same argument the project makes for verifiers. --strict gives the one-off run where every owed signal fails.

Writing check.* is the act config:permissions under the roles decision proposed beside this one.

## Consequences

The kind vocabulary already exists (ank check --kind refuses an unknown one and lists them); this publishes which of them may be raised.
