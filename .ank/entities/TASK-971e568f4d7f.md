A binding needs a declaration at both ends (LOG-9a80124cc6ad), and forgetting one
is silent: docs/peers.md shows `ank context src/` in `web` answering "no ready
tasks in scope" with nothing else while `api` already scopes an ADR into it. An
orchestrator bootstrapping two repositories will make that mistake, and nothing
will tell it.

Each end can see the other once its own declaration resolves, so each end can
name what the other is missing. It is a signal and never a fault: the fix lives
in another repository. Comparing "resolves to me" is the identity comparison of
TASK-d1996aa9851d, hence the order.
