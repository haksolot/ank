TASK-daf25ab8a9b7 has been `in_progress` since commit 98dbf3b with no ref under
`refs/ank/claims/`. `context` lists it as live work, so nobody picks it up, and
nothing anywhere says it is not held. Fifteen signals on this corpus and not one
of them is about the task that is lying.

`check` already knows how to notice the neighbouring case: a claim present but
expired signals "claim by X expired: the task is claimable again"
(`human.rs`, in the `Record::Claim` arm). The gap is the arm that does not
exist — a status saying held with no record to hold it. The two are the same
invariant read from opposite sides, and only one of them is implemented.

Signal, never fault. A lapsed claim is a stale record and the corpus is intact;
failing CI over it would teach the reader that `check` exits 8 for reasons that
do not matter, which is the fastest way to make exit 8 mean nothing.

The scope covers `check` and the integration test rather than a fix to
TASK-daf25ab8a9b7 itself. Releasing that task by hand corrects today and
nothing else; the missing checker is the defect.
